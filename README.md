# Meal Pix

A colorful meal planner. Save meals with photos and ingredients, shuffle lunches and dinners for the week, plan for up to 4 people, and get one combined grocery list.

**Open it:** https://3fivesf.github.io/meal-pix/

## On iPhone
Open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen and works offline.

## Your data
Without a household, everything you add (meals, photos, weeks, names) is saved only in your own browser. Use **Back up** and **Restore** at the bottom of the app to move it.

## Share with family
Tap **Share with family** at the bottom of the app to start a household, then send the invite code or link. Everyone in a household shares meals, photos, weeks and the grocery list. Each household is private to its members. The shared data is stored in Supabase; `supabase-setup.sql` sets up the tables and access rules.
