# Meal Pix

A colorful meal planner. Save meals with photos and ingredients, shuffle lunches and dinners for the week, plan for up to 4 people, and get one combined grocery list.

**Open it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/meal-pix/

## On iPhone
Open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen and works offline.

## Your data
Everything you add (meals, photos, weeks, names) is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and other people who open the link get their own empty copy.
Use **Back up** at the bottom of the app to save a file of your data, and **Restore** to load it on another device or after clearing your browser.
